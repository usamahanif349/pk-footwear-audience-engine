FAQ

Questions brands ask

How does the free pilot work?

The first 3 brands get 30 days of the Growth plan for free: the buyers to answer, alerts, the Monday email and a walkthrough call with your team. No payment details needed. At the end we ask for 15 minutes of honest feedback and, if you're happy, permission to mention you as a pilot brand.

Where does the data come from?

Public YouTube comments, collected through the official YouTube Data API: brand channels plus creators' sale vlogs, hauls and reviews. Instagram, TikTok and Facebook aren't included because their terms don't allow this kind of collection.

How fresh is it?

An automatic job runs every Monday: it collects new comments, re-runs the analysis and republishes the site. The date at the top of the app shows the latest update.

How is "missed sales" calculated?

Unanswered buying questions × the share who'd buy if answered × the average pair price. The defaults are 10% and Rs 4,500, and you can change both under Assumptions in the app. It's an estimate, not a measured figure.

Can it read Roman Urdu?

Yes. Word lists in English, Roman Urdu and Urdu tag clear cases, and a multilingual AI embedding model tags similar comments the lists miss. Spelling varies a lot, so a small share stays unlabelled; the app says how many.

What about privacy?

Commenter names are never stored. Phone numbers, e-mails, @handles and order numbers are removed before analysis.

Are you working with these brands?

No. Joota Pulse is independent. Brand names are used only to describe public comments about them, and every brand sees the same public data.

Do we need to install anything?

No. Your team opens a link. Leads, alerts and the weekly digest are ready every Monday, and the client report downloads as a PDF.
